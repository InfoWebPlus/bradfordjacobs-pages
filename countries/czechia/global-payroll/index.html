What payroll in Czechia costs

Your statutory employer costs, plus a payroll partner fee. The Czechia's employer employer social contributions and auto-enrolment add real detail; the value of the partner is accuracy and time.

Employer NI

15% NI above the local currency 5,000 threshold, plus the 3% pension auto-enrolment minimum (eligible employers get some NI back via the Employment Allowance).

Minimum wage (2026)

National Living Wage is local currency 12.71 an hour for 21 and over from April 2026, with lower bands for under-21s and apprentices.

Income tax (employee)

Progressive at 0%, 20%, 40% and 45% across the 2026/27 bands, withheld through local payroll tax. Borne by the employee, calculated and filed correctly each pay run.

Payroll partner fee

A transparent per-employee or per-payslip fee. We benchmark across our vetted network, typically 15 to 25% below going direct.
